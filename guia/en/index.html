How it works, in plain language

The five parts of the service

🪪 Registration

It's the business's ID card in AI. You search for the business in the Business Center and create a profile, or claim one that already exists ("claim" means asking to be recognized as the owner of a profile ChatGPT created on its own).

🔐 Verification

It's like having a document notarized. A line in the "DNS" (the control panel where the website address is set up) proves that the client owns the website. Then the owner confirms their identity.

📋 Business profile

Name, website, category, and a description of up to 300 characters, kept the same on the website, Google, and social media. Consistent information everywhere makes things clearer for AI.

🌐 Several AI tools

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity read the internet in different ways. You check all four, not just one.

📈 Monitoring

Every month, run the same diagnostic to compare with the previous month. When the Business Center dashboard (mentions and clicks) becomes available to the client, it goes into the report. The dashboard opens after identity verification; ours was approved on 23/11/2026, and the numbers will appear here once the dashboard has data.

🚫 What it does not promise

The Business Center is in beta, and registering does not guarantee a recommendation or the top spot. You sell organization and monitoring.

Get recommended

Listing corrects who the business is. Recommendation is a different job

OpenAI’s help says that claiming the profile does not guarantee greater visibility or give you control over ChatGPT recommendations, and that ads do not change the answers. “We’ll make ChatGPT recommend your business” does not belong in the listing proposal. Recommendations are driven by the second layer of the service.

1. What AI reads about the client

Recommendations come from what AI finds elsewhere: reviews, local lists and guides, and website pages that answer, in simple language, the exact questions your client’s customers ask.

2. Business listed in search engines

AI reads the internet that search engines already know. Add and verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap, and check what’s indexed. It’s free and included in the checklist.

3. Sell it as before and after

The 10 questions a customer would ask AI, with screenshots of the answers; do the work and repeat the same questions in 60 days. The kit’s diagnosis is already your “before.”

4. The method: inemaSEO

inemaSEO covers this layer: SEO (Google and Bing), GEO (getting cited by AI), AEO (pages that answer questions), and WebMCP (a site ready for AI agents), with scripts for Search Console, Bing, and IndexNow. To study it step by step: inemaSEO Training.

Measure

Measure without asking by hand every week: that is what turns into a monthly fee

The kit's medir.py asks the questions on its own every week and warns you when something gets worse. The client doesn't pay the monthly fee for the sign-up: they pay to watch the curve climb month after month and to know right away when an AI goes back to swapping their company for another one. Combine three measures, from the most reliable to the least.

1. Visits that arrive from the AIs

Real data, and free. ChatGPT tags the links it shows with utm_source=chatgpt.com. In the client's Google Analytics, under Acquisition › Session source, you'll see chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com. Export the month's CSV and the kit adds up the visits per AI.

2. The Business Center dashboard

The dashboard itself shows mentions, clicks to the site and the topics where the company appears. It only covers ChatGPT and only shows numbers when there are enough conversations. It takes two minutes a month: copy it into the report.

3. The questions, asked automatically

medir.py asks the AIs the 4 diagnostic questions, 3 times each (the answer changes from one time to the next), applies the same score and keeps the history. It uses your subscription to Claude Code, Codex or Gemini (Antigravity), with no API key. OpenRouter is optional.

The three commands (the first one, every week):

python3 kit/medir.py rodar kit/cliente.json --ias claude,codex,gemini --vezes 3
python3 kit/medir.py relatorio kit/cliente.json --saida mes.md   # exits 1 when there is an alert
python3 kit/medir.py visitas origens-do-mes.csv                  # CSV exported from Analytics

⏰ Leave it running on its own every Monday

On a computer or server that stays on, open the scheduler with crontab -e and paste the 4 lines found in the README, replacing VOCE with your username. First, run which claude codex agy python3 and put the folders that appear on the PATH line; without that the scheduler can't find the AIs. Each week is saved in relatorios/AAAA-MM-DD.md. The email alert (MAILTO) only arrives if the machine has a mail server configured, which a personal computer or an ordinary VPS almost never has. Without it, open the report every Monday or ask Claude Code to swap the alert for a Telegram message. crontab exists on Linux and macOS; on Windows, install WSL (Linux inside Windows) and do the same there. Check on the first Monday that the file showed up.

Cost: 36 questions with search per client per week (3 AIs × 4 questions × 3 times), about 5 to 10 minutes, within your subscription. With many clients, spread the days out so you don't hit the usage limit.

Common questions

Before you sell

Do I need to code?

No. For the diagnostic, just ask questions and take notes in a browser. The DNS record goes in the control panel of the company that sold the domain (Registro.br, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Cloudflare…); the companion kit shows you exactly where.

How much should I charge?

The kit does not make up a market price. It gives you a calculation: estimated hours × your hourly rate, with room for surprises, plus a monthly fee for monitoring.

Can I guarantee the business will be recommended?

No. The Business Center is in beta, and OpenAI does not promise a position. Guarantee what you deliver: an official profile, verification in progress, correct details, and a report. To get recommendations, sell the second layer: Get recommended.

Do I need the client's password?

No. Ask for editor access to the DNS panel or work together on the client's screen. The owner always confirms their own identity.

Is this only for ChatGPT?

The official registration is with ChatGPT. The diagnostic and consistent profile details also help with Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity.

Does it cost anything?

The kit and registration are free. An agent (Claude Code or Codex) is optional and requires a separate paid plan.
